   - Source: developer.nlr.gov Alternative Fuel Stations API.

   - Store: station ID, location, network, port count, connector, power, public access, operating status, source URL, retrieval date.

2. Utah DOT AADT traffic data
   - Source: UDOT Traffic Statistics / AADT history and map data.
   - Why second: the proposal needs a single road-segment traffic fact, and UDOT publishes the relevant AADT history rather than requiring a complex live-data pipeline.
   - Store: UDOT segment ID, road name, AADT, data year, direction/segment metadata, source URL, retrieval date, reviewer.
   - Implementation shape: start with a controlled import for the road segment serving the property; automate refresh after the annual UDOT update.

   - Source: Utah State Tax Commission Vehicle Registrations - Recent Data.
   - Why third: official files are linked by year, require no vendor contract, and directly support Utah County BEV-growth inputs.
   - Store: geography, registration year, vehicle/fuel category, count, source file URL, retrieval date, and any transformation/version metadata.
   - Implementation shape: download and normalize the annual files, with a review step for format changes.
   - Caveat: this is a file-ingestion workflow, not a low-latency API.
