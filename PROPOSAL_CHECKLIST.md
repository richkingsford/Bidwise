## Deliberate MVP assumptions

- Bidwise keeps numbers illustrative until a utility bill, interval data, roof plan, and site design are supplied.
- Tax incentives, VPP revenue, charger utilization, and retail foot-traffic revenue are shown as opportunities, not guarantees.
- Section-level pencils now replace the former edit drawer: click a pencil in a section heading, edit the highlighted text inline, and click the checkmark to finish.
